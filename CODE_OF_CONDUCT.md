# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and clear. Welcome different backgrounds and experience. Critique ideas and code, not people.

Harassment, threats, hate, sexual harassment, doxxing, intimidation and unwanted contact are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or ban participants according to severity and patterns of behavior, explaining decisions when safe.

Report concerns to maintainer [alebgl77](https://github.com/alebgl77). If no private channel is available, request one without naming affected people or publishing personal details. Reports will be handled discreetly where possible; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Maintainers follow the same standards and consider good-faith appeals. This is a project-specific policy, not a claimed verbatim copy of another covenant.
